Welcome Makers

Campus Make Club is open to all students who enjoy building things — electronics, woodworking, 3D printing, code, crafts, or anything in between. No experience needed, just curiosity.

We meet Thursdays at 6 pm in the Engineering Makerspace, Room 104. Bring a project or just show up.

Meetings & Projects

  • Weekly Build Nights

    Every Thursday. Open shop time — work on anything. Mentors welcome.

  • Monthly Mini-Project

    One guided build per month (e.g., line-following robot, lamp, stool).

  • Show & Tell

    Last meeting each month — demo your work, get feedback, share tips.

  • Tool Training

    Safety intros for laser cutter, CNC, soldering stations, 3D printers.

How to Join

Drop in any Thursday — no sign-up required. For updates and the mini-project schedule, join our channels:

  • makeclub@university.edu
  • Discord
  • Instagram

Questions? Email the officers or DM us on Discord. First meeting is always free — just bring yourself.
